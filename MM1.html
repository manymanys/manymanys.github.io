Study overview

From insects to humans, learning helps animals respond to changing environments.

Reversal learning asks what happens when a previously learned association changes. It offers a way to investigate how animals adapt their behavior, but differences between studies can make comparisons across species difficult.

ManyManys 1 is developing and implementing a shared task to measure, describe, and compare reversal learning abilities across animal taxa. By collecting data from a wide range of species in a single study, we aim to better understand learning and its evolutionary origins.
