We are thrilled to announce that as of this summer, ManyManys has appointed a new Research Community Officer! Angela Koçiu is an undergraduate student specializing in psychology at Concordia University with an interest in cognition as well as developmental psychology. 

During her time at ManyManys, she will mostly be doing administrative work and supporting the research community through communication and coordination. Through this work, she will be contributing to the ManyManys1 Reversal Learning Project. 

Angela was drawn to ManyManys because of her newfound interest in animal cognition. She is fascinated by its comparative approach, which offers broader insights into how cognitive abilities develop, including in humans.

In particular, Angela is grateful for the opportunity to gain hands-on experience in the research field by contributing to this inspiring Open and Big Team Science project.

Outside of research, she enjoys baking, exercising and reading. 

Angela is excited to join the ManyManys community and to see where this experience takes her!
