As part of our open science event series, we are excited to launch our upcoming webinar: 

**Open research tools: Behavioral coding using BORIS**

📅 Date & time: October 14, 11:00 a.m. – 1:00 p.m. (ET) on Zoom

🎤 Facilitators: Miranda Gómez Díaz and Alexandra Paquette

🔗 Register here: [https://forms.fillout.com/t/vxUL6eDpbKus](Register here: https://forms.fillout.com/t/vxUL6eDpbKus)

📅 [Calendar invite](https://calendar.app.google/xUH5BjjKWH1uXUVF7) (with Zoom link)

🔗 [Zoom link](https://concordia-ca.zoom.us/j/83998201434?pwd=0j6vzImY2rvaBt8EwnaMLeiY4pNJTI.1)

Join us for a practical introduction to BORIS (Behavioral Observation Research Interactive Software) and its use in ManyManys1 behavioral coding! We’ll walk through the basics of setting up a project, coding MM1 behaviors using the ethogram, exporting data, and assessing inter-rater reliability, with examples tailored to the MM1 task.

This workshop is intended for anyone involved in behavioral coding for MM1 and will be a great opportunity to get familiar with the coding process and ask questions before getting started. Your participation will also help us maintain consistency in behavioral coding across the project.

We’re looking forward to seeing you there! Please feel free to share this invitation with colleagues and students involved in MM1 behavioral coding.

For any questions, please contact us at themanymanys1@gmail.com.
