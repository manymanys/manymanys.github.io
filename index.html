About ManyManys

ManyManys connects researchers across disciplines, institutions, and countries to develop shared methods for comparative cognition. By working together, we can study a broader range of species and build more robust, reproducible research.

Founded following the Big Team Science Conference in 2022, our network brings existing collaborations and individual researchers into a shared scientific effort.
